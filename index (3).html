01 / The Regulatory Emergency

The 2027 legal compliance window leaves no room for spreadsheet drift.

Under ICPAK's binding regulatory roadmap, IFRS S1 and S2 requirements apply directly to Kenyan Public Interest Entities (PIEs) for reporting periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027. True corporate assurance requires absolute proof. Yet, your core operational data remains scattered across physical fuel card receipts, unintegrated generator runtime logs, and vendor invoices that were never engineered to withstand an external financial audit.

01 —

Data Fragmentation

Isolated Operational Silos: Telemetry arrives across disparate facilities in erratic intervals and unstandardized formats. Consolidating this manually into standard financial reports is slow, brittle, and impossible to repeat reliably.

02 —

The Verification Vacuum

Unverifiable Disclosures: When an aggregate emission number is decoupled from its physical source document, your enterprise cannot prove its validity. If an auditor cannot trace it, your capital access faces immediate risk.

03 —

Regulatory Exposure

The Cost of Manual Entry: Financial controllers and external consultants waste hundreds of hours re-keying historical logs by hand. Structural errors and calculation drift are buried silently until the final audit exposes them.

02 / The Technical Architecture

AI extracts. Plain code calculates. Audits clear.

Ataraxia replaces manual human data entry with an unalterable processing pipeline. We cleanly segregate artificial intelligence from mathematical calculation, ensuring complete defensibility.

01 /

Dynamic Document Ingestion

Vision-capable models ingest your historical utility bills, fuel manifests, and remote asset telemetry. It extracts precise consumption metrics and captures the exact verbatim text snippet where the value resides.

02 /

Programmatic Threshold Auditing

Our deterministic rules-engine cross-checks the data for chronological gaps, double-billings, and mathematical anomalies. Any value lacking explicit evidence or clear human sign-off is instantly isolated.

03 /

Cryptographic Traceability

Every verified metric is irreversibly tied to its original document via unique file hashes (SHA-256). Estimates and data holes are clearly segregated and flagged with specific uncertainty bounds—never blended with hard evidence.

04 /

The Evidence Dossier

The system exports an impeccably formatted, beautifully structured compliance portfolio for your executive board and external assurance providers. The foundational emissions mathematics is written in immutable, open code—never an AI model's guess.

04 / Architectural Principles

Integrity by design.

Radical Traceability

The lineage connecting a raw physical asset record, its computational transformation, and the final corporate disclosure must remain completely transparent and open to inspection.

Deterministic Math

We banish black-box calculations. AI is utilized solely to read text; plain, versioned, open-source code executes all calculations. The engineering remains fully inspectable.

Absolute Human Oversight

Automation streamlines the verification pipeline; it never replaces professional judgment. A named corporate officer must review and sign off on every validated ledger state.
